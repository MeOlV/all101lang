Me pēhea te whakamāori i tāu ake pukapuka ki ngā reo 101 i roto i ngā meneti

вересня 24, 2026

Nā te taputapu Gemini me te kaiwhakamāori paerewa i roto i ngā Tuhinga a Google, kua taea e au te whakarahi ake i ngā tuhinga ki te taumata ao tata tonu, me te iti rawa o te utu. Nā taku ake tuhinga tukatuka puranga, ka tukuna e au he ratonga hei whakamāori tere i tō tuhinga ki roto i te neke atu i te 100 reo, me te kore e hiahiatia he wawaotanga ā-ringa. He aha i mahi ai: ⬧ Tino tere: 101 ngā reo i roto i te 15 meneti (≈ 9 hēkona mō ia reo mō tētahi tuhinga 48,000 kupu). ⬧ Tauine: Tiakina ngā pane, ngā rārangi, ngā ripanga me ngā whakaahua. ⬧ Penapena: Ko te utu mō te whakamāori i tētahi pukapuka ki ngā reo 101 he tata ki te €5 / $6 noa iho. Kei raro nei ngā tohutohu me ngā tikanga katoa mō te tono i te whakamāoritanga 101 o tāu mahi.
