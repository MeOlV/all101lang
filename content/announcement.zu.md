Ithuluzi le-Gemini kanye nomhumushi ojwayelekile ku-Google Docs kungivumele ukuthi ngikhulise imibhalo iye ezingeni lomhlaba wonke cishe ngokushesha futhi ngezindleko eziphansi. Ngenxa yesikripthi sami sokucubungula amaqoqo, nginikeza isevisi yokuhumusha ngokushesha idokhumenti yakho ezilimini ezingaphezu kwe-100 ngaphandle kokungenelela ngesandla.  
  
Kungani kusebenza:  
  
<!--more-->  
  
* Ishesha kakhulu: Izilimi eziyi-101 ngemizuzu eyi-15 (≈ imizuzwana eyi-9 ngolimi ngalunye kudokhumenti enamagama angu-48,000).  
  
* Ukukwazi Ukukhula: Gcina izihloko, uhlu, amathebula, nezithombe.  
  
* Ukonga: Izindleko zokuhumusha incwadi ngezilimi eziyi-101 zingama-€5 kuphela / $6.  
  
Ngezansi uzothola imiyalelo ephelele kanye nemigomo yoku-oda ukuhumusha okungu-101 komsebenzi wakho.
