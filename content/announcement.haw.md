ʻO ka mea hana Gemini a me ka unuhi maʻamau ma Google Docs ua ʻae iaʻu e hoʻonui i nā kikokikona i kahi pae honua kokoke koke a me ke kumukūʻai liʻiliʻi. Mahalo i kaʻu palapala hana batch ponoʻī, hāʻawi wau i kahi lawelawe e unuhi koke i kāu palapala i nā ʻōlelo he 100 me ka ʻole o ke komo lima ʻana.  
  
No ke aha e hana ai:  
  
<!--more-->  
  
* Wikiwiki loa: 101 mau ʻōlelo i loko o 15 mau minuke (≈ 9 kekona no kēlā me kēia ʻōlelo no kahi palapala 48,000 huaʻōlelo).  
  
* Scalability: Mālama i nā poʻomanaʻo, nā papa inoa, nā papa, a me nā kiʻi.  
  
* Mālama kālā: ʻO ke kumukūʻai o ka unuhi ʻana i kahi puke i 101 mau ʻōlelo he €5 / $6 wale nō.  
  
Ma lalo iho nei e ʻike ʻoe i nā kuhikuhi piha a me nā huaʻōlelo no ke kauoha ʻana i ka unuhi 101 o kāu hana.
